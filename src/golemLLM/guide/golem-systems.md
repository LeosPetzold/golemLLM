# GOLEM systems: what each subsystem does and how it shows up in data

GOLEM is a small limiter tokamak at FNSPE CTU in Prague, built from the former TM-1/CASTOR machine. Its main use is education and small-scale plasma experiments. Physics background is in `guide://tokamak-primer`.

## 1. Machine parameters (check against the current wiki and recent shots)

Sources disagree on operating numbers, and they have risen over time:
- Major radius R and limiter radius a: read the current values from the operator's notes; the values change between sources.
- Toroidal field, plasma current and pulse length: the operating limits differ between sources and change over time. Use the operator's current limits; the wiki design targets are wider than the operating limits.
- Toroidal field generated by discharging a capacitor bank into 28 coils, with an iron transformer core.

When a limit in one source conflicts with another, use the most conservative one and tell the operator.

## 2. Subsystems and the Conductor modules that drive them

Each row: the subsystem, what it does physically, and the Conductor module or parameter that controls it. Field names are from `parameters/` and `quantities/` in `conductor/discharge.json`.

**Toroidal field (Bt).** A capacitor bank (`infrastructure.toroidal_magnetic_field`) is charged to `voltage_v` and crowbarred into the Bt coils at the `bt` trigger time (`bt_delay_us`). The pick-up coil (`toroidal_field_derivative`) measures dB/dt; the field is the integral, calibrated by K_BtCoil. `hold_for_trigger` keeps the bank charged for the trigger instead of dumping it at target voltage (0 means charge-only rehearsal).

**Electric field / ECD (Et).** The primary transformer bank (`infrastructure.toroidal_electric_field`) drives the loop voltage around the core. `connect_primary` connects it to the transformer primary at trigger. Setting it to 0 is a charge-only rehearsal. The loop voltage (`loop_voltage`) is the single most important signal of the shot.

**Capacitor charging.** One shared source charges all banks (`infrastructure.capacitor_charging`). The source is set to the highest bank target plus a headroom. Banks must read near zero (within `quiescent_voltage_tolerance_v`) before charging, or preparation refuses.

**Triggering.** A commissioning trigger path (`infrastructure.triggering`) fires the bt, ecd and das lines at their delays (microseconds from the sequence start). Timing errors show up as a shifted or missing breakdown in the loop voltage and the trigger channel (`discharge_trigger`).

**Working gas.** A pressure regulator (RVC 300, `infrastructure.working_gas`) sets the fill pressure before the shot, in millipascal. The shot is refused if the measured pressure before firing is outside `tolerance_mpa` of the request. Gas is hydrogen (H2) in the usual operation; helium is used for cleaning (see `guide://wall-conditioning`).

**Position stabilisation.** Radial and vertical field waveforms (`infrastructure.position_stabilization`) are programmed as time-value pairs (`time_us,value;...`). Power supplies (Kepco racks), function generators and scopes are involved. The main switch must be on for any waveform to run. Waveforms must end with 0 (`guide://basics`).

**Current stabilisation.** A waveform (`infrastructure.current_stabilization`) programs the current set point over time. Same waveform format and rule as above.

**Chamber and vacuum.** Rotary and turbo pumps, and vent valves, keep the vessel at base pressure. Chamber pressure in mPa and chamber temperature are logged; forevacuum pressure in Pa is checked before pumping. See `guide://session-control`.

**Diagnostics.** Basic diagnostics (loop voltage, Rogowski coil for dI/dt, Bt pick-up, photodiode, diamagnetic loop, trigger channel), interferometry (line-averaged density), and fast cameras. Mirnov coils, probes (Langmuir, ball-pen, double rake, scintillation) and spectrometers appear in the shot database for some sessions. See `guide://reading-shots`.

## 3. Sequence of one discharge (Conductor)

1. Preparing: checks (bank voltages near zero, pressures in range, modules executable). Failure here stops everything before energy is stored.
2. Arming: set points written (bank voltages, currents, gas pressure, stabilisation) and read back.
3. Charging: shared source charges banks; each bank reports its own charging curve.
4. Executing: trigger sequence fires; diagnostics record for `record_duration_ms` (read the length from the setup record) with `pretrigger_ms` before the trigger.
5. Outcome and evidence are written to `discharge.json`.

Timing matters: the Bt coil must be active before the ECD breakdown, and the stabilisation waveforms must match the plasma phase. A plasma that breaks down before the field is ready is a timing problem, not a physics result.

## 4. Known constraints

- Maximum bank voltage and maximum working-gas pressure: read from the setup document in the record (`plan/setup_document`).
- Current stabilisation and position stabilisation have a maximum amplitude per channel listed in `guide://basics` (LIMITS).
- A bank whose `quiescent_voltage_tolerance_v` is exceeded refuses preparation. Do not work around it by changing the tolerance.

## 5. What to check when a subsystem looks wrong

- Bt low or late: bank voltage read-back, `bt_delay_us`, `hold_for_trigger`, pick-up coil quality.
- No breakdown: loop voltage peak, Ecd bank voltage, `connect_primary`, gas pressure before firing, trigger timing.
- Plasma current starts then collapses: loop voltage fall-off, density trend, stabilisation saturation.
- Position drifts: radial/vertical waveform saturation, generator and scope readings.
- Pressure refused: `pressure_predischarge_mpa` vs `requested_pressure_mpa` difference.
